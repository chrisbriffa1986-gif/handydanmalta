About

Meet Dan.

Most home jobs take five phone calls: finding someone, chasing them, waiting for them to turn up, chasing them again. HandyDan takes one.

No shop, no showroom: the van is the base, so Dan comes to you anywhere in Malta. He does the work himself, agrees everything before he starts, and leaves your home the way he found it. Bathrooms and plumbing are his speciality; mounting, installations and assembly are the everyday jobs he fits around them.

Questions

Before you call.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Malta. Gozo by arrangement for larger jobs.

Yes, by arrangement. Say what suits you when you call and Dan will tell you what's possible that week.

Do you supply the parts?

Either. Dan can source the parts, or fit parts you have already bought.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. If something Dan fitted or fixed fails because of the work, he comes back and puts it right.

Does Nella come to every job?

Only when invited. She is excellent at supervising and terrible at holding a spirit level.
